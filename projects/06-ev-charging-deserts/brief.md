# Brief: charging access gaps

**Status:** outline — Days 12–13.

## Decision

_Where a public L2/DCFC siting program would close the largest access × equity gap in the study metro._

## Question

Which census tracts have poor public charging access *and* low income (and, if ACS allows, high vehicle dependence)?

## Method

AFDC points, 1 km buffers (and nearest DCFC). ACS denominators. Worst-quartile access ∩ bottom-quartile income.

## Results

_Map + tract list._

## Limitations

Buffers ≠ drive time; home charging is unobserved; AFDC completeness varies by network.
