# Evidence note: pooled effects and cost per outcome

**Status:** outline — fill on Days 3–4.

## Decision

_Is the pooled effect large enough, given cost and heterogeneity, to treat this as a funding-relevant intervention in the settings studied?_

## Question and pre-registered-ish rules

Outcome, population, study design included, and what “too heterogeneous to pool” means (e.g. I² > 75% *and* prediction interval crossing zero) — write this before looking at the forest plot.

## Method

Random-effects meta-analysis; prediction interval; optional leave-one-out. Cost per outcome = cost per person / (effect × baseline), with Monte Carlo on both pieces.

## Results

_Forest plot, I², pooled effect, cost per outcome._

## Limitations

Publication bias, outcome switching, 3ie coverage vs unpublished nulls.
